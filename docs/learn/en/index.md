## Start here {#category-0}

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Install Gnosi and take your first steps](getting-started.md)**

    Create a small research workspace, capture an idea and find it again. You can complete this journey without AI or an external account.

- **[Workspaces, Vaults and sharing](workspaces.md)**

    A workspace determines the people and permissions you work with. A Vault holds the knowledge files. Switching one can change the records and settings you see.

</div>

## Research and knowledge {#category-1}

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Create pages, links and attachments](pages-files.md)**

    Use pages for your own ideas and keep the supporting material close to them. Gnosi stores knowledge in ordinary Markdown and YAML files.

- **[Organize records and plan work](databases-planning.md)**

    A database groups tables. A table defines properties for page records; its views show the same records in different ways.

- **[Explore links and create a genogram](graph-genograms.md)**

    The knowledge graph shows links between your material. A genogram represents people and explicitly recorded family relationships.

- **[Capture sources, read and cite](reading-references.md)**

    Follow the research chain from a source to evidence and then to your own writing. Feed reading and reference management are separate features.

- **[Ask questions about selected sources](notebooks.md)**

    A notebook groups selected reference records so that you can ask questions grounded in their attachments and URLs.

- **[Configure an agent and a model](agents-models.md)**

    A model generates responses. An agent combines a model with instructions and assigned skills; tools let it perform specific actions.

</div>

## Connected work {#category-2}

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Use mail, calendar and contacts](mail-calendar-contacts.md)**

    Connect the accounts you need and keep track of which source owns each message, event or contact.

- **[Prepare media and publish content](publishing-media.md)**

    The media center holds reusable assets. Social publishing prepares and sends content to the networks you configure.

- **[Enable plugins, connect services and automate](integrations-automations.md)**

    Plugins make optional features available in a Vault. Integrations connect accounts. Automations run configured operations on a schedule.

</div>

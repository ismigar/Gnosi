## Description

Gnosi is a private, local-first workspace for notes, documents, tasks, calendars, references and AI assistance. It works directly with Markdown files, runs on your own machine or server, and avoids vendor lock-in. Gnosi is a self-hostable, open-source alternative to Notion and Obsidian for individuals, researchers, teams and cooperatives.
